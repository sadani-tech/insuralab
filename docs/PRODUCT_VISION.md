# InsuraLab product vision
InsuraLab helps people understand protection with clarity, evidence, and room to think.

The public product combines a navigable knowledge map, plain-language concepts, editorial answers, practical guides, and source context. Its core works without AI or embeddings. Optional AI may explain approved material but cannot become a gate to learning.

The visual experience should feel calm, professional, and protective: warm surfaces, navy text, restrained teal, careful hierarchy, and honest source labels. Avoid fear, aggressive sales, invented credentials, and implied guarantees.

A future private agent workspace supports preparation, activity tracking, applications, policy service, and claim assistance. The current revamp prepares that boundary and its blueprint. It does not collect prospect or customer records.

General concepts remain distinct from provider-specific studies. Provider identity is data, not the identity of InsuraLab. Unknown source rights and unreviewed product facts must remain explicit. See REVAMP_PLAN.md for current implementation and ROADMAP.md for subsequent work.
