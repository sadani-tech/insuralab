# Protection Needs Assessment Methodology

Status: implemented in Phase 7  
Methodology version: **1.0**

## Purpose

The Protection Needs Assessment helps a visitor reflect on financial responsibilities, income dependency, existing protection, emergency preparedness, and the possible financial impact of major life events. Its output is a ranked map of **insurance topics worth learning**, not a purchase recommendation.

The intended journey is:

`situation → financial exposure → protection topic → why it may matter → general learning content`

## Non-goals

The assessment does not:

- diagnose medical or actuarial risk;
- estimate underwriting eligibility;
- determine whether existing coverage is adequate;
- calculate premiums, quotations, sums assured, or a “protection gap”;
- provide personal financial planning;
- recommend an insurer or product;
- use AI, machine learning, probability, or actuarial models.

## Privacy principles

- No login is required.
- Questions do not request names, contact details, identity numbers, addresses, employer names, policy numbers, diagnoses, medical records, medication, disease history, exact salary, debt amount, or bank balance.
- Financial context uses broad ranges.
- Laravel evaluates answer keys in memory. There are no response or answer tables and no detailed answers are persisted.
- The browser stores only the normalized result in `sessionStorage` so the result route works without putting answers in the URL or storing them indefinitely.
- Phase 7 adds no analytics system.

## Dimensions

Version 1.0 uses five focused dimensions:

1. **Life Protection** — financial impact of death on dependants and ongoing obligations.
2. **Health Protection** — possible impact of medical costs and understanding existing medical-expense protection.
3. **Critical Illness Protection** — impact of serious illness on income and non-medical recovery needs.
4. **Disability Protection** — impact of a permanent reduction in earning ability.
5. **Accident Protection** — financial consequences of accidental death or bodily injury.

The dimensions belong to the assessment definition. They are not products and have no provider relationship.

## Question philosophy

Version 1.0 has eleven configured questions; ten apply when the visitor has no dependants. The dependant-health question is the only conditional branch. Questions use plain Bahasa Indonesia, single-choice radio semantics, and range-based financial context. “Tidak tahu” is a valid statement of uncertainty and never carries the maximum weight.

The accident question asks whether the visitor wants to understand financial impact. It does not infer occupational or lifestyle risk. Serious-illness questions ask about financial impact and never ask about diagnosis or medical history.

## Evaluation model

The engine is deterministic and database-configured:

1. The API resolves each semantic question and option key against the published assessment.
2. It rejects duplicates, unknown keys, mismatched options, missing required answers, and answers to inapplicable questions.
3. A selected option can have zero or more `assessment_option_impacts`.
4. Each impact names one assessment dimension, a bounded weight from 0–5, a stable reason code, and an editorial Indonesian explanation.
5. Weights and reasons are never accepted from the browser and are not exposed in the public definition.
6. The same definition version and answer set always produce the same result.

No executable code is stored in the database. Configuration consists of structured rows and constrained scalar values.

## Normalization

Dimensions have different possible scoring ceilings. For each dimension, Laravel computes:

`normalized relevance = earned impact / maximum impact across applicable questions`

Only applicable questions contribute to the denominator. Raw and normalized numeric scores remain internal; the public response exposes only a calm learning-relevance tier.

## Relevance levels

- `0 to < 0.30` → **Foundational / Fondasi Pembelajaran**
- `0.30 to < 0.60` → **Worth Exploring / Layak Dieksplorasi**
- `0.60 to 1.00` → **Highly Relevant / Sangat Relevan**

Dimensions are ordered by tier, then normalized relevance, then editorial order. Foundational dimensions remain visible. The visualization never labels a low tier as bad, unsafe, deficient, or underinsured.

## Reason system

Every non-zero impact may carry a stable code such as `HAS_DEPENDENTS`, `PRIMARY_INCOME_SOURCE`, `LIMITED_EMERGENCY_BUFFER`, `UNSURE_HEALTH_COVERAGE`, or `LIMITED_ALTERNATIVE_INCOME`. The API returns the editorial consumer-facing message. A dimension with no selected impacts receives a neutral foundational explanation.

Reasons are written by editors and are never generated by AI. Changing a reason does not change arithmetic unless its weight is separately changed.

## Learning recommendations

Each dimension has an ordered many-to-many relation to published **general** articles. An option can also link to additional general articles—for example, policy-reading material when a visitor has coverage but low confidence in understanding it.

The Eloquent relations enforce `provider_id IS NULL` and public publication rules. Missing or unpublished articles are omitted safely. Product IDs, product slugs, and provider recommendations are not part of the result contract.

Existing BPJS, employer, private, or overlapping coverage is treated as meaningful existing protection. The assessment encourages understanding benefits, limits, service networks, exclusions, claims, coordination, and sustainability; it does not declare those sources sufficient or insufficient.

## Provider neutrality

The engine reads assessment dimensions, impacts, reasons, and general article relations. It never queries products and filters learning articles to general knowledge. Automated tests assert that provider-specific content and product-recommendation fields are absent.

## Limitations

- The assessment is an educational snapshot based on self-reported broad choices.
- It does not account for the full context needed for personal financial advice.
- A tier means “relevant to learn,” not likelihood, severity, eligibility, or adequacy.
- Refreshing midway restarts the questionnaire in the MVP.
- The result is browser-session only and is not shareable or downloadable.

## Methodology versioning

The assessment carries `methodology_version`. The frontend sends the displayed version during evaluation. Laravel rejects stale submissions so answers are not silently evaluated against changed rules. A future rule or question change must increment this version. Full historical replay is deliberately deferred.

## Testing personas

- **Persona A:** single, no dependants, strong emergency buffer, existing health coverage — life must not become artificially high.
- **Persona B:** primary/sole earner with dependants and obligations — life learning is highly relevant.
- **Persona C:** employer/BPJS coverage with low policy understanding — existing protection is respected and policy education is shown.
- **Persona D:** no dependants but severe income interruption and no alternative income — disability is highly relevant while life is not forced high.
- **Persona E:** frequent “Tidak tahu” — foundational learning is encouraged without maximum relevance.

The API suite also covers malformed payloads, conditions, determinism, thresholds, missing articles, provider neutrality, and the absence of response persistence.
