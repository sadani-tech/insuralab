# Current editorial rules
Write in clear Indonesian, with a concept, example or practical steps, limitations, and a source that can be opened.

New introductory text is first-party editorial material and explicitly awaits independent expert review. Never create a reviewed date or imply a human signed off when that did not happen. Source access rights are separate: unreviewed, public, restricted, personal.

Public source approval must concern the particular material/usage. A public product page does not make an internal playbook public. Preserve source version and location, paraphrase within permitted usage, and do not redistribute local PDFs.

The map's reading connections are labelled as editorial associations, not causal or contractual guarantees. Keep the explanation and supporting passage together. Editing a cited passage requires a new version.

FAQ answers are persisted editorial text. Low-confidence searches return candidate materials or no results; they do not synthesize a response and pretend to answer the question. Guides/checklists are complete without AI.

Provider benefits, prices, eligibility, commissions, and career rules are time-dependent and require current authorized evidence. Internal sales promises are not facts. Do not imply that an agent makes insurer underwriting/claim decisions. Refer to the archived guide for older writing conventions only where consistent with these rules.
